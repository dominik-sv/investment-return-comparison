How long you hold changes everything

US stocks vs. cash, every start date from July 1926 to the end of 2025, pooled by how long you hold. Overlapping windows built from Ken French (market, 1‑month T‑bill) and BLS CPI.

How this works

What you're looking at

For every day you could have started investing (July 1926 to the end of 2025), we compute what your money did if you held for a chosen number of years, then pool all of those outcomes into one distribution. Move the slider to change the holding period. Each outcome is a window return — the total change in value over that window, in %.

The windows overlap heavily, so at long horizons only a handful of them are truly independent — read the far right of the fan chart with caution.

What "market" means

The total return of the whole US stock market — every US-listed company, weighted by size — from the Ken French data library (CRSP universe, Mkt-RF + RF), daily since 1926. Dividends are reinvested. It's broader than the S&P 500 but moves almost identically.

What "risk-free rate" means

The return on 1-month US Treasury bills (Ken French RF, monthly). We assume you buy a new bill each month and hold it to maturity — no gain is booked in between rolls, which is slightly conservative and prices in the illiquidity.

Real vs. excess

  • Real — adjusted for inflation (US CPI), i.e. change in purchasing power.
  • Excess — the market minus what T-bills earned over the same window: the extra you got for taking the risk.

The charts

  • Fan — the spread of outcomes by holding period. Darker = more likely; the coloured line is the median (the typical outcome). The key under it names each shade band by its percentile range.
  • Distribution (PDF / CDF) — every outcome at the selected horizon. PDF: how common each result is. CDF: the chance of ending at or below a given value. The worst 10% are  red, the best 10%  teal; the black line is the mean.
  • Chance of a loss — the share of windows that ended below where you started.
  • Tiles — mean, spread (standard deviation) and the 10th / 90th percentile at the selected horizon.

Buttons

  • View — excess-over-cash, or real market vs. real cash side by side.
  • Total / Per year (real view only) — show each window's return as its total change, or as the equivalent annual rate. Per-year makes the distributions roughly comparable across horizons — you can watch the spread tighten the longer you hold.
  • Auto fit / Lock — the distribution x-axis reframes to fit each horizon as you slide. Hit Lock to freeze the current frame, then scrub the slider to watch the pile move inside it. Anything that falls off the sides is counted in the "◂ %" / "% ▸" labels.
  • PDF / CDF — switch the distribution panels between a histogram and a cumulative curve.
  • Click anywhere on the fan chart to jump the horizon there.
